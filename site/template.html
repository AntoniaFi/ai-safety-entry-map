AI Safety Entry Map

Research areas you can enter with your current skills

By Antonia Fichtbauer · source on GitHub

I am a bioprocess scientist pivoting into AI safety, and like many new to the field I wanted to understand where someone like me can do useful research now, without spending a year on education first. I tried to answer this by creating a targeted literature analysis which focuses on the methods used across AI safety problem areas and the authors backgrounds. This analysis is based on thousands of AI-safety papers and their requirements: coding skill, maths, compute, human involvement.

I hope this tool will be useful to some.
